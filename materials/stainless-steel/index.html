When to choose stainless steel

Stainless steel is useful where corrosion resistance and an exposed metal surface matter. Typical flat parts include cover plates, panels, fixtures and decorative profiles. Choose the grade and finish to suit where the part will be used and how it needs to look.

  • Exposed cover plates and flat panels
  • Fixtures and mounting profiles
  • Decorative parts where the metal face remains visible

Need a particular grade or type?

If you need a particular stainless grade, such as 304 or 316, or a material certificate, tell us before ordering. We can confirm whether the grade and surface finish you need are available.

Hole sizes, part sizes and fit

Our usual cutting tolerance is ±0.2 mm: the finished size can be slightly larger or smaller than the drawing. Larger parts or complex shapes can vary more. If a hole or edge must fit another part precisely, discuss it with us before ordering. Surface finish and cut edges

Stainless steel is corrosion resistant, but the environment, grade and surface condition still matter. A cut edge can look different from the sheet face. If a visible face must have a particular brushed direction, polish or protective film, discuss it before ordering.

Deburring is included: we remove burrs from the metal edges. If you need a particular edge shape, finish or precise fit, tell us before ordering. Our quality and tolerances guide explains what to check.

Textured powder coating is available for eligible metal parts. Check the colours and options shown in your quote.

Stainless steel questions

Can I specify a brushed or cosmetic face?

Tell us which side will be visible and how it needs to look. If a brushed finish is required, tell us the grain direction. The illustration does not show the exact surface of your order, so confirm any appearance requirements with us first.

Which stainless steel grade will I receive?

Ask us to confirm the grade for your chosen thickness before ordering if your design requires one. Tell us if you also need a material certificate.

Can you match a brushed finish?

Send the required finish, the visible face and any grain direction with your drawing. Check availability before ordering if appearance is important.

Is stainless steel suitable outdoors?

Suitability depends on the grade and exposure. Tell us whether the part will see weather, salt, chemicals or another demanding environment so the specification can be checked.
