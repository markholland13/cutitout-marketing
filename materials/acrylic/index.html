When to choose acrylic

Acrylic is a non-metal option for signs, display components and decorative panels. Choose clear or coloured sheet to suit the appearance you want. Keep enough material around fixing holes and between cut-outs to avoid fragile sections.

  • Sign shapes and cut-out lettering
  • Display components and decorative panels
  • Clear or tinted profiles with internal cut-outs

Available thicknesses

These thicknesses are in our range. Your quote shows which options are currently available for your part.

  • 3 mm
  • 5 mm

Need a particular acrylic type?

Acrylic is made in different types, including cast and extruded sheet. If your design needs a particular type, brand, clarity or protective film, tell us before ordering so we can check availability.

Hole sizes, part sizes and fit

Our usual cutting tolerance is ±0.2 mm: the finished size can be slightly larger or smaller than the drawing. Larger parts or complex shapes can vary more. If a hole or edge must fit another part precisely, discuss it with us before ordering. The table shows the smallest holes and slots we can cut; a hole for a bolt or pin may need to be larger to give the right fit.

Surface finish and cut edges

Clear, black, white and dark grey tinted acrylic are listed in our range. Your quote confirms current availability. The cut edge and sheet face can have different appearances, and lighting changes how clear or tinted material looks. If a visual match matters, contact us before ordering.

Acrylic is cut on a CO₂ laser and does not need deburring. If you need a particular appearance or precise fit, tell us before ordering. Our quality and tolerances guide explains what to check.

Give acrylic fixings some room

Avoid forcing fasteners into close-fitting holes or leaving very narrow connections. Check your assembly method and tell us about any critical feature.

Acrylic questions

Will the acrylic edge be deburred?

No. Our acrylic is cut on a CO₂ laser and does not need deburring. Check that fixing holes have enough room for your fasteners and that narrow sections will be strong enough for your use.

What acrylic colours are available?

The listed range includes black, white, clear and dark grey tinted acrylic in 3 mm and 5 mm thicknesses. Your quote confirms available combinations.

Can acrylic be powder coated?

The optional powder coating service is for supported metal parts. Choose the acrylic sheet colour and appearance you want instead.

Can you cut acrylic lettering from a logo?

Yes, if the artwork can be prepared as clear cutting outlines. For cut-out lettering, add bridges to hold any centres you want to keep, such as the middle of an ‘O’. Check small details against the acrylic cutting limits.
