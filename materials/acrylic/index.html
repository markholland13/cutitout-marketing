When to choose acrylic

Acrylic is a non-metal option for signs, display components and decorative panels. Choose between clear or coloured sheet around the appearance you need. It behaves differently from metal: allow care around fixing holes, corners and narrow connections.

  • Sign shapes and cut-out lettering
  • Display components and decorative panels
  • Clear or tinted profiles with internal cut-outs

Grade and material specification

Cast and extruded acrylic can behave differently during cutting and later processing. If your design needs a specific type, brand, optical appearance or protective film, confirm that specification before ordering. The colour and thickness list does not promise a particular manufacturing type.

Design limits and fit

Our usual cutting tolerance is ±0.2 mm; larger parts or complex geometry can vary more. Confirm critical fits before ordering. The minimum hole or slot size and overall part limits change with sheet thickness. These are cutting limits, not a guarantee of fit for a bolt, bearing or locating pin.

Surface and finishing

Clear, black, white and dark grey tinted acrylic are listed in our range. Your quote confirms current availability. The cut edge and sheet face can have different appearances, and lighting changes how clear or tinted material looks. If a visual match matters, contact us before ordering.

Parts are supplied deburred. Discuss critical dimensions, any additional edge preparation and appearance before ordering. Our quality and tolerances guide explains the details to consider.

Give acrylic fixings some room

Avoid forcing fasteners into close-fitting holes or leaving very narrow connections. Check your assembly method and tell us about any critical feature.

Acrylic questions

What acrylic colours are available?

The listed range includes black, white, clear and dark grey tinted acrylic in 3 mm and 5 mm thicknesses. Your quote confirms available combinations.

Can acrylic be powder coated?

The optional powder coating service is for supported metal parts. Choose acrylic around the sheet colour and appearance you need.

Can you cut acrylic lettering from a logo?

Yes, if the artwork can be prepared as suitable cutting paths. Decide which centres should stay connected, add bridges where needed, and check small features against the acrylic limits.
