When to choose aluminium

Aluminium is a useful option for lightweight panels, covers, spacers and enclosure profiles. It weighs less than a steel part of the same size. Changing material also changes stiffness and how the part behaves, so check that it still meets your design requirements.

  • Lightweight cover plates and panels
  • Enclosure profiles and spacers
  • Prototype parts where low weight matters

Need a particular grade or type?

The alloy is the type of aluminium; the temper describes how it has been treated. Both affect strength and suitability for bending or welding. If your drawing specifies an alloy or temper, send us the details so we can check availability before ordering.

Hole sizes, part sizes and fit

Our usual cutting tolerance is ±0.2 mm: the finished size can be slightly larger or smaller than the drawing. Larger parts or complex shapes can vary more. If a hole or edge must fit another part precisely, discuss it with us before ordering. The table shows the smallest holes and slots we can cut; a hole for a bolt or pin may need to be larger to give the right fit.

Surface finish and cut edges

The sheet face and laser-cut edge have different appearances. Handling marks can matter on a visible panel, particularly if it is supplied without a coating. Tell us about surfaces that will be visible, intended finishing and any protective-film requirement before ordering.

Deburring is included: we remove burrs from the metal edges. If you need a particular edge shape, finish or precise fit, tell us before ordering. Our quality and tolerances guide explains what to check.

Textured powder coating is available for eligible metal parts. Check the colours and options shown in your quote.

Aluminium questions

Can I order a 3 mm aluminium plate?

Yes: 3 mm is one of the listed aluminium thicknesses, alongside 1, 1.5 and 2 mm. Use the table above to check features and overall size. If you need a particular alloy or plan to bend the part later, check those requirements with us before ordering.

Which aluminium alloy do you stock?

Ask us to confirm the alloy for your chosen thickness if your design requires one. Include any temper requirement from your drawing.

Can my aluminium part be powder coated?

Coating is available on supported metal jobs. Check the options in your quote and discuss the intended finish before ordering if it is critical.

Can I upload a folded aluminium STEP model?

Suitable sheet-metal models can be prepared as flat cutting drawings. We do not offer bending: your parts are supplied flat and deburred, so plan forming separately.
