When to choose aluminium

Aluminium is a useful option for lightweight panels, covers, spacers and enclosure profiles. It weighs less than a similarly sized steel part, but changing material can also change stiffness, surface behaviour and suitability for the job. Confirm those requirements in your design.

  • Lightweight cover plates and panels
  • Enclosure profiles and spacers
  • Prototype parts where mass matters

Grade and material specification

Aluminium alloy and temper affect properties such as strength, bendability and welding behaviour. If the part will be formed, welded or used in a demanding application, specify the alloy and temper you need and confirm availability. Do not infer an alloy from the thickness list.

Design limits and fit

Our usual cutting tolerance is ±0.2 mm; larger parts or complex geometry can vary more. Confirm critical fits before ordering. The minimum hole or slot size and overall part limits change with sheet thickness. These are cutting limits, not a guarantee of fit for a bolt, bearing or locating pin.

Surface and finishing

The sheet face and laser-cut edge have different appearances. Handling marks can matter on a visible panel, particularly if it is supplied without a coating. Tell us about cosmetic faces, intended finishing and any protective-film requirement before ordering.

Parts are supplied deburred. Discuss critical dimensions, any additional edge preparation and appearance before ordering. Our quality and tolerances guide explains the details to consider.

Explore textured powder coating for eligible parts. Available colours and options are shown in your quote.

Aluminium questions

Which aluminium alloy do you stock?

Send any alloy and temper requirement before ordering. The online range lists material and thickness; a specific alloy needs confirmation.

Can my aluminium part be powder coated?

Coating is available on supported metal jobs. Check the options in your quote and discuss the intended finish before ordering if it is critical.

Can I upload a folded aluminium STEP model?

Suitable sheet-metal models can be prepared as flat cutting drawings. We do not offer bending: your parts are supplied flat and deburred, so plan forming separately.
