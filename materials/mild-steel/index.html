When to choose mild steel

Mild steel is a practical starting point for general fabrication where a painted or coated finish is planned. It suits flat brackets, mounting plates, spacers and panels. A thicker sheet may give more rigidity, but it also increases weight and changes the minimum features you can cut.

  • Mounting plates with fixing holes
  • Flat brackets and general fabricated profiles
  • Spacers, panels and prototype parts

Grade and material specification

A material name alone does not specify a steel grade or surface condition. If your drawing calls for a particular grade, certificate, pickled surface or other requirement, confirm it with us before paying. The listed online thicknesses do not imply that every specification is stocked.

Design limits and fit

Our usual cutting tolerance is ±0.2 mm; larger parts or complex geometry can vary more. Confirm critical fits before ordering. The minimum hole or slot size and overall part limits change with sheet thickness. These are cutting limits, not a guarantee of fit for a bolt, bearing or locating pin.

Surface and finishing

Uncoated mild steel can rust when exposed to moisture. Plan suitable protection for the environment where your part will be used. If the part will be welded or painted, tell us about any surface preparation that matters to your process.

Parts are supplied deburred. Discuss critical dimensions, any additional edge preparation and appearance before ordering. Our quality and tolerances guide explains the details to consider.

Mild steel questions

Can I powder coat mild steel?

Textured powder coating is available on supported metal jobs. Check the options for your part and discuss the intended environment if corrosion protection is important.

Will my mild steel part arrive rust-proof?

An uncoated mild steel part needs suitable protection for its environment. Choose an available finish or plan your own preparation and coating.

Can you cut a single steel part?

Yes. Set the quantity to one when quoting. You can compare the price with a batch before ordering.
