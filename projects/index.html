What we can cut

Detailed profiles

This order combines a shaped outline with small holes, long slots and triangular cut-outs. All of that detail is cut from the customer’s drawing.

Internal cut-outs

The outside shape is only part of the job. Here we’ve cut the large centre holes and tapered openings along with the rounded outer profile.

Repeat parts

Several of the same part, cut to the same drawing. The deep slots and rounded corners are repeated across the whole batch.

A mix of shapes and sizes

Long profiles and smaller pieces, cut in matching sets. We can cut several different designs in one order, so you can get the parts you need together.

Tell us about your project

A useful enquiry starts with the cutting file and the part’s purpose. Add the material, thickness, quantity and any finish you are considering. Tell us about a critical fit, a visible face or a date you need to meet.

If the file is ready, our online laser cutting service lets you compare the price of one part with a small batch. If something needs checking, talk to us before ordering.

Built around practical projects

Cut It Out began with the need for custom parts while building a race car. The difficulty of getting one-off parts led to a small CNC plasma table, followed by work for other people and investment in an industrial fibre laser.

That experience is behind the emphasis on single parts, small runs and straightforward quoting. Read the workshop story and meet Cut It Out.

Share the finished project

If we have made parts for your project and you would like to share the result, use our contact button to send a photograph and a few details. Tell us which images and information you are happy for us to use.

An installed part helps other customers understand the result: show the part in place, describe what it does and explain which details mattered to your project.
