Powder coating changes the finished surface. Think about the assembly and appearance while preparing the drawing, before the part is cut.

Identify the surfaces that must fit

Coating adds a layer to the metal. Look at holes, slots, sliding surfaces, edges that locate another part and close-fitting assembly faces. The bare-metal clearance may need to change when the part is coated.

Tell us about functional fits before ordering. Agree the required finished dimension and whether allowance or masking is appropriate, rather than assuming a universal coating thickness.

Mark any area that must remain uncoated

If a hole, thread, contact area or assembly face must stay bare, include that requirement in your enquiry. Do not assume that masking is included just because a finish has been selected.

  • Provide a separate annotated reference showing the areas to discuss.
  • Keep the uploaded cutting DXF limited to intended cut geometry.
  • Confirm the masking arrangement and any additional work before paying.

A clear reference helps explain the requirement without adding unwanted lines to the cutting file.

Decide which faces will be visible

Identify cosmetic faces and any edge where appearance matters. Choose your colour from the options available for the job. Cut It Out’s stocked coating range is textured; the black plate on the site is an illustration of that type of finish.

If you need a colour match, a particular texture or an agreed appearance standard, discuss the specification before ordering. Lighting and screens are not reliable approval samples.

Describe the intended environment

Tell us about outdoor exposure, chemicals, abrasion, heat or another demanding condition. The base metal and coating specification need to suit the intended use.

For a particular corrosion or durability requirement, check the specification with us before selecting the finish. Do not rely on a general coating description as a performance guarantee.

Check a finished first part

For a new assembly, a coated first part lets you assess fit and appearance in the real application. Confirm important dimensions before manufacture, then check the physical result before committing to more.

If you change the drawing or material after that check, upload and quote the revised file. Keep the confirmed finish and any masking requirements with the order details.

Before selecting the finish

  1. Check that coating is available for your chosen metal job.
  2. Identify critical holes, mating faces and visible surfaces.
  3. Agree any masking or specific finish requirement.
  4. Confirm timing if your order has a deadline.
  5. Review the full specification before paying.
