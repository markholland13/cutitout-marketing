Guides / Cost & quantities

Make the part work.
Make the drawing efficient.

The price depends on your actual drawing and order choices. These checks help you compare sensible options without losing the features your part needs.

Remove geometry you do not need

A cutting file should contain the outside profile and intended cut-outs. Dimensions, page borders, notes, spare views and duplicate lines can make the file harder to interpret and may add unwanted geometry.

Keep each intended cut once. File Workshop can help identify duplicate paths, but compare any prepared file with your original before accepting changes.

Simplify details that serve no purpose

Every hole, slot and intricate edge adds geometry. Ask whether a decorative cut-out or very detailed contour is needed for the function or appearance of the part. A simpler approved design may be easier to manufacture.

Keep the features that make the part work

Do not remove necessary fixing holes, weaken connections or change a functional fit to chase a lower price. Compare revised drawings against the original requirements.

Compare listed materials and thicknesses

Start with the listed material and thickness range. Special grades or finishes may need a separate availability check. Changing material or thickness can affect weight, rigidity, corrosion and the smallest features you can cut.

Quote the actual alternatives that meet your design requirements. The cheapest sheet choice is only useful if it remains suitable for the finished part.

Compare one part with a batch

Single parts are welcome, with a £20 minimum order. Set the quantity to one, then compare the quantity you expect to need. The quote shows how your choices affect the price for your design; do not assume a fixed discount.

For a new design, a first part can help you check fit before committing to more. If you revise the drawing after that check, upload the new file and quote it again.

Read about prototypes and small batches.

Choose finishing around the job

Optional finishing changes the order specification. Select powder coating when it serves your appearance or use requirements, and check any masking or additional work with us before paying.

We are not VAT registered and do not charge VAT. Review the full order total, including shipping. A useful comparison keeps material, thickness, quantity and finish consistent between quotes.

Put the checks into practice

  1. Keep a copy of the original design.
  2. Clean unused and duplicate geometry.
  3. Compare only materials and thicknesses suitable for the job.
  4. Try the quantities you actually need.
  5. Check the preview, specification and complete order total.
