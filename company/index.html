01 / How it started

It started
with a race car.

Cut It Out grew from a practical problem. While building a race car, many of the parts needed to be custom made, and waiting for laser cut parts was slowing the project down. Because I was often only looking for one-off parts, many companies would not even get back to me.

That led me to build a small 4 ft x 4 ft CNC plasma table so I could cut my own parts. From there, I started making parts for other people, and the work eventually grew to the point where investing in an industrial fibre laser made sense.

02 / Why we built this

Less chasing.
More making.

This website was built to make custom parts easier to order, especially when you only need one-offs or smaller runs and do not want to spend days chasing emails just to get a price.

It lets you quote and order custom laser cut parts online, review earlier orders, and track delivery updates in one place. If you need help with a drawing, material choice, or repeat order, you can still get in touch directly.
